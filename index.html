About Me

I am an Agricultural Statistician based at the Horticulture Research Institute (HRI) of the Kenya Agricultural and Livestock Research Organization (KALRO). My work focuses on turning field and on-station experimental data into clear, actionable insights that support researchers, extension officers and farmers.

Over the years I have provided statistical support across a wide range of commodities and systems — banana and plantain evaluation, legume biological nitrogen fixation, biofertilizer trials, Dorper sheep finishing rations, poultry performance, market studies and large baseline surveys. I routinely design experiments (RCBD, CRD, split-plot, factorial), analyse data using ANOVA, ANCOVA, mixed models, logistic regression and non-parametric methods, and prepare technical reports and manuscripts.

I am passionate about capacity building and have conducted statistics training for researchers and contributed to farmer sensitization and technology transfer projects funded by partners such as the European Union, KAFACI and others.
