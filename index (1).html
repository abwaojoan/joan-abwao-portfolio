01 - About

Turning data into
actionable insights

I am an Agricultural Statistician at the Kenya Agricultural and Livestock Research Organization - Horticulture Research Institute (KALRO-HRI).

My work focuses on designing experiments, analysing field and on-station data, producing technical reports, and supporting researchers and farmers with clear statistical evidence.

I work across banana & plantain evaluation, biological nitrogen fixation, biofertilizer trials, Dorper sheep finishing rations, poultry performance, market studies, KAP surveys and multi-county GIS study-site mapping.
